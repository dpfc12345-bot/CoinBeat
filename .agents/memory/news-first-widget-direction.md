---
name: News-first widget direction
description: Market Pulse widget product and visual-direction decision.
---

Market Pulse is a Coinness-driven crypto news product for Korean customers, not a price-tracking dashboard. Its widgets and app surfaces should foreground Korean news headlines, Korean source/relative-time labels, category, and affected-asset context; prices and charts are optional supporting details, not the lead.

**Why:** The creator explicitly rejected trading-dashboard presentation and generic AI-looking typography. The desired experience is a restrained, editorial news surface that helps users understand what is happening before opening the app.

**How to apply:** For home-screen widgets and notifications, use a Korean-friendly system sans stack, clear headline hierarchy, generous spacing, quiet warm-neutral surfaces, and a single restrained lime accent for live/selection. Avoid monospaced all-caps labels, dense metric grids, charts, fake score gauges, neon gradients, and price-first layouts unless a later requirement explicitly asks for them.
