---
name: News-first widget direction
description: Market Pulse widget product and visual-direction decision.
---

Market Pulse is a Coinness-driven crypto news product for Korean customers, not a price-tracking dashboard. Its widgets and app surfaces should foreground Korean news headlines, Korean source/relative-time labels, category, and affected-asset context; prices and charts are optional supporting details, not the lead. The current visual direction is clean deep navy with electric-blue accents.

**Why:** The creator explicitly rejected trading-dashboard presentation and generic AI-looking typography, then selected the attached clean blue Upbit-like reference. The desired experience is a restrained, editorial news surface that helps users understand what is happening before opening the app.

**How to apply:** For home-screen widgets and notifications, use a Korean-friendly sans stack, clear headline hierarchy, generous spacing, deep navy surfaces, and restrained electric-blue selection/live accents. Avoid over-neon effects, fake metric cards, and price-first layouts; a large widget may combine a concise price snapshot with one dominant Korean headline.
